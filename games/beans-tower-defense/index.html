HOW TO PLAY

Balloons float along the road. If one reaches the bean house at the end, you lose lives. Stronger balloons cost more lives.

Click a bean in the shop on the right, then click on the grass to place it. The circle shows its range: green means you can place it there, red means you can not. Right-click cancels.

Click a bean on the map to see its stats and upgrade it with one button (up to 10 times; upgrades 5 and 10 give it a new look), change what it aims at, or sell it again. Press SELECT at the bottom left to pick several beans and upgrade them all at once.

Farms grow money every wave. Upgrade them to grow more. Buy better farms and new beans in the Bean Shop with Bean Coins. You can bring 7 beans.

A boss balloon comes every 10 rounds, and the last round brings the King Balloon. In Infinity mode the waves never end, but they get harder and harder.

You earn money for every balloon layer you pop and for every wave you finish.

Press START WAVE when you are ready. Pause, speed up the game (1x, 2x, 3x) or turn on Auto to start every wave by itself. Zoom with the scroll wheel, the + and - buttons or two fingers. Drag to move the camera when you are zoomed in.
