HOW TO PLAY

Goal: destroy the enemy BEAN CORE in their base. Towers protect each lane: you must destroy the outer tower before the inner one, then the base tower. When a base tower falls, the two guard towers next to the core can be attacked, and when both guards are gone the core is open.

Moving and attacking: Right-click the ground to walk, right-click an enemy to attack it. A + click = attack-move, S = stop.

Abilities: Q W E R aim at your mouse. Every level gives an ability point: press Shift+Q/W/E/R or click the yellow +. The ultimate (R) unlocks at level 6, 11 and 16.

Gold: land the last hit on minions and monsters, defeat enemy heroes and towers. Press P in your base to buy items. Cheap parts combine into stronger items. Press B to recall to base and heal.

Jungle: monsters between the lanes give gold and XP. The Brainfreeze Yeti and Chili Golem give buffs. The Sugar Dragon gives your whole team a permanent bonus. The Mega Bean gives a strong team buff — bring friends!

Bushes: standing in a bush hides you from enemies outside it. Areas without allies nearby are covered in fog.

Camera: mouse wheel zooms, Space centres on your bean, Y locks/unlocks the camera, click the minimap to look around. Tab shows the scoreboard.
